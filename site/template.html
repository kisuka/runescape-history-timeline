RuneScape population timeline

Monthly average concurrent players for RuneScape and Old School RuneScape from the game's launch in January 2001 to __LAST_MONTH__, with every month labelled. Scroll the chart sideways. Updates are marked above the plot for OSRS and below it for the main game and Jagex-wide items.

Drag the chart or hold Shift and scroll to move sideways. Pre-2013 values come from archived snapshots of Jagex's player counter, which skew toward peak hours; months with no snapshot are bridged with a dotted line (most of 2001-2003, plus Nov 2005 and Aug 2007).

Source for every event · Player counts: aggrgtr.com (continuous tracker from Feb 2013; Wayback Machine reconstruction before that). Events: Jagex news posts, OSRS Wiki, GamerBlurb, PC Gamer, Checkpoint Gaming, and the leaked 2011 Jagex botting memo.
